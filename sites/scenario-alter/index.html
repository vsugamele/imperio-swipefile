How it works

Three modes.

Every change starts from what has to stay. Pick the mode by what must stay exactly as it is.

Swap

Keep everything. Change one thing.

A product in a hand, a prop, a sign or an outfit.
The person, the light and the camera stay as they are.

Motion transfer

Keep the performance and the camera. Change who or where.

A new lead or a new place for the same take.
The timing, the moves and the framing carry over.

Restyle

Keep the motion and the framing. Change the whole look.

Anime, a 3D feature or claymation over the same take.
Every move stays where it was.

From take to keepers

  1. 1

    Probe

    Read the clip first: length, frame rate, size and sound. Clips over 30 seconds are split into parts.

  2. 2

    Pick a preset

    Start from one of 36 presets for the three modes, or describe the change in plain words.

  3. 3

    References

    Add pictures of the product, the outfit, the person or the place.

  4. 4

    Target frame

    One still of the change, photographed into this exact frame, guides the edit.

  5. 5

    Run a 480p draft

    See the change at low resolution before paying for the full render.

  6. 6

    Check

    The draft is measured against the source: length, change, structure, motion, frozen frames.

  7. 7

    Finalise the keepers

    Only the drafts that pass go to full resolution.

  8. 8

    Put the sound back

    The source sound goes back on, so lips and steps stay in sync. A new place gets its own ambience.

The realism pass

Printed, not pasted.

Swap a product using only a flat product shot and the model lays it on top of the take: the old shape survives and the print floats. Photograph the new can into one frame of the shot first, give the model that frame, and every frame comes back with a real can in his hand. Same take, same prompt, two references.

Quality check

Checked before you see it.

Every draft is measured against the take before anyone sees it, and every keeper again after it is finalised. The numbers for each version sit in the panel next to the player. Then a person looks at the crops.

The checker measures

Length
The same number of frames as the source.
How much changed
The share of the frame that differs from the take.
Structure kept
How much of the layout and the edges survives.
Motion match
Whether things move the way they moved in the take.
Jumps
A pose that resets or morphs between two frames.
Tail drift
The last frames wandering off the take, so they can be trimmed on every version alike.
Frozen frames
Stalls and repeated frames.

Only eyes catch

Brand marks
A real logo the model was never asked for.
Hands
Fingers, grip and what the hand is holding.
Text
Signs and labels that turn into nonsense.

The skill

Alter yours.

The skill gives your agent the whole method: the three modes, the presets, the realism pass, the checks and the local scripts. Point it at your own clip.

What's inside

  • SKILL.md the method, mode by mode
  • 36 presets for swap, motion transfer and restyle
  • The realism pass for products that look printed, not pasted
  • The QA guide what to measure and what to look at
  • The sound and MCP guides what to keep, what to make, how to run it
  • 9 local scripts prep, prompt, check, finish, version reels and long clips
