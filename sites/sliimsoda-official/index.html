WHAT IS IN SLIMSODA

The four things inside every SlimSoda scoop

SlimSoda is built around four components. Three of them do the work; the fourth exists so the other three survive the trip. Read the label on your tub before you start — it is the authoritative list.

The main active

Bioactive berberine in SlimSoda

Berberine is a plant alkaloid and one of the most studied natural compounds in this category. Its well-documented weakness is absorption: a peer-reviewed pharmacokinetic review puts oral bioavailability below 1%, largely because of extensive first-pass metabolism. That is the problem the rest of the formula is built around.

The support active

Concentrated gingerol in SlimSoda

Gingerol is the pungent compound in fresh ginger, used in kitchens and traditional preparations long before it reached a supplement label. It is included here as a companion to the berberine rather than as a standalone actor.

The cellular component

NAD+ in SlimSoda

NAD+ is a coenzyme present in every cell and involved in energy metabolism and cellular repair. Levels are known to decline with age, which is why it appears in formulas aimed at adults over 40.

The delivery vehicle

Sodium bicarbonate in SlimSoda

Plain baking soda — and the reason the product is a powder you stir into water rather than a capsule. It acts as an alkaline buffer, raising stomach pH at the moment the dose passes through, which is the manufacturer’s stated rationale for the format. It is the vehicle, not an active.

On the source above: the under-1% figure comes from a 2022 review in Phytotherapy Research on berberine pharmacokinetics. It describes berberine in general, not SlimSoda, and it measures absorption — not any outcome in people who take this product. The delivery rationale is the manufacturer’s claim and has not been evaluated by the FDA.
