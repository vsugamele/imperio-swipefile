What It Is

Nutritional Support for Normal Glucose Metabolism

Sugar Defender is a dietary supplement intended to support normal glucose metabolism and healthy blood sugar levels already within the normal range.

Blood glucose can be influenced by many factors, including food choices, physical activity, sleep, stress, medications, and medical conditions. A dietary supplement should be used only as one part of an overall wellness plan.

Important medical notice: Sugar Defender is not a treatment for diabetes and is not a substitute for prescribed medication, glucose monitoring, medical nutrition therapy, or care from a licensed healthcare professional.

Adults who have diabetes or prediabetes, use insulin or glucose-lowering medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition should speak with a qualified healthcare professional before using any dietary supplement. Do not change or stop prescribed treatment based on information on this website.
