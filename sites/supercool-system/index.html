    FAQ

    01

    What does $7 actually get me — and what does it cost after?

    Straight answer: $7 gets you full access to the platform plus enough starter credits to take a real idea through its first stages — a screenplay, your first generated scenes, a poster. Enough to know, tonight, whether this is real. It does not get you a finished feature film; generating a full movie uses more credits, and credits cost money. Anyone who tells you an entire film costs seven dollars is lying to you. What we'll tell you instead: it costs a fraction of what a camera crew, editor, or composer costs — and you can see the quality for yourself before spending another cent.

    02

    Will anyone actually watch an AI film? Isn't there a stigma?

    Some people will dismiss your film the moment they hear "AI" — we won't pretend otherwise. But audiences at large don't buy tickets for production methods; they show up for stories. AI-made films are pulling real view counts on YouTube and streaming platforms right now, and the viewers aren't asking how the frames were rendered. The stigma is loudest among people who make films the old way — not among people who watch them. You're early. That's usually uncomfortable, and usually the advantage.

    03

    Can I actually make money from this?

    We won't promise you income — nobody honestly can, and film has never worked that way. Here's what's true: most traditional indie films lose money because they cost tens of thousands to make before a single ticket sells. When your production cost is a subscription and credits, the math changes completely — breaking even stops being a miracle. We teach the real distribution paths (YouTube, Prime Video Direct, Vimeo On Demand, niche licensing) and how members monetize them. Whether your film earns depends on your story, your niche, and your effort. What we remove is the part that used to make profit impossible: the cost.

    04

    Is my idea safe? Will other people see my script?

    Your projects are private to your account. Your screenplays, scenes, and story bibles aren't published, shared with other members, or shown anywhere unless you release them. And since ideas aren't protectable anyway — execution is — the best protection for your idea has always been finishing it first. That's the one thing this platform is genuinely built to make happen.

    05

    AI is changing so fast — shouldn't I just wait until it's better?

    The models will absolutely be better next year. They'll also be better the year after that — there is no finish line to wait for. Two things don't change while you wait: your story doesn't write itself, and the people starting now are building the skills, the catalog, and the audience you'll be competing with later. When the models improve, they improve inside the platform — you don't rebuy anything. The directors who win in five years are the ones with five years of finished films, not the ones who waited for the tools to be perfect.

    06

    Where does the AI still fall short? Be honest.

    Fair question, honest answer. Long, dialogue-heavy scenes take iteration to get right. Perfect physical continuity across many shots — the exact coffee cup in the exact spot — still requires attention, just like a real set. And if you're a frame-by-frame perfectionist, you'll spend time refining, not one-click generating. What it does brilliantly: short films, trailers, documentaries, music videos, and features where you direct in passes — generate, review, refine. It's not magic. It's a crew that works at the speed of your decisions, and that's never existed before.

    07

    How does the 30-day guarantee actually work?

    One email. Within 30 days of joining, tell us you want your money back and you get it — no exit survey, no phone call, no "let me transfer you to retention." You keep everything you created and everything you learned. We can offer that because most people who type their idea in on night one don't want their $7 back — they want more credits. But the door out is real, it's simple, and it's yours.
